STRIDE — Transition Smoothness & Continuity

Anonymous supplementary material for a double-blind submission. Each row shows the same start→end transition rendered by four methods from identical boundary frames. Ours (STRIDE) is compared against three pretrained-prior baselines. Videos are muted and each plays once when scrolled into view; use Replay this comparison under a row to watch all four again in sync.

What this page illustrates. These clips support the paper's smoothness / temporal-continuity finding: baselines frequently produce an abrupt cut (the start frame is held, then the end scene jumps in) or leave the background frozen, whereas STRIDE renders a continuously evolving transition. Judge the rows on continuity of motion over time, not on per-frame photorealism.

Selection. These are illustrative examples drawn from our 48-clip benchmark, not a uniform sample. The complete quantitative comparison across all 48 clips — including the dimensions and categories on which STRIDE underperforms the baselines — is reported in the paper's Results and Limitations sections.
